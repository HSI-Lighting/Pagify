# Privacy Policy for Pagify

**Effective date:** 2 September 2026
**Applies to:** Pagify for iOS and Pagify for Android

## Summary

Pagify is an offline PDF reader and editor. It has no account system, no server,
and no analytics. Your documents are opened, annotated and saved on your own
device and are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

We do not collect **any** personal data. There is nothing for us to sell, share,
or hand to a third party, because nothing ever reaches us.

## What we collect

Nothing. Pagify has no user accounts, no sign-in, no telemetry, no crash
reporting and no advertising. We do not collect your name, email address, phone
number, location, contacts, device identifiers, or usage statistics.

## Your documents

Every PDF you open stays on your device. Rendering, text extraction, annotation
and page editing are all performed locally by the app's own engine. Edited files
are written back to the location you chose.

A document leaves the app only when you explicitly send it somewhere — using the
iOS or Android share sheet, saving a copy through the system file picker, or
copying text to the clipboard. In each case the operating system, not Pagify,
delivers it to the destination you picked, and Pagify keeps no record of it.

## Network access

**Pagify for iOS makes no network requests of any kind.** The app contains no
networking code: no HTTP client, no sockets, and no embedded web browser. It has
no App Transport Security exceptions and declares no background network activity.

**Pagify for Android does not request the `INTERNET` permission**, so the
operating system prevents the app from accessing the network at all.

Neither app can send your documents anywhere, by design rather than by promise.

## Permissions

**iOS** — Pagify requests no runtime permissions. It does not access the camera,
the photo library, your contacts, your location, your microphone, or your health
data. Documents reach the app only when you hand one to it, through the Files
app, the share sheet, or another app opening a PDF in Pagify.

**Android** — Pagify declares one permission, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, used
solely to save documents you have edited on older Android versions that require
it for shared storage.

## Third-party components

**Pagify for iOS** bundles PDFium, an open-source PDF rendering engine. It runs
entirely on your device, has no network capability, and sends nothing anywhere.
The iOS app contains no other third-party SDK — no advertising, analytics,
attribution or crash-reporting library of any kind.

**Pagify for Android** bundles PDFium and, additionally, **Google ML Kit Text
Recognition** for optical character recognition. ML Kit runs fully on-device in
offline mode; no image, page or recognised text is sent to Google or to any other
server. This feature is present only in the Android app. The iOS app performs no
text recognition. See Google's terms at
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

## Required-reason APIs (iOS)

As required by Apple, Pagify's privacy manifest declares its use of file
timestamp APIs (reason code C617.1). These are used to sort your recent documents
by when they were last modified. This information stays on your device.

## Children's privacy

Pagify is rated suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it
collects no data from children.

## Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to retain and nothing to delete.
Removing the app removes its local files from your device.
